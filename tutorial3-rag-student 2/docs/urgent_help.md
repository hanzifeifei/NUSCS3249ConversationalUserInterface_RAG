# Urgent help

## The clinic is not an emergency service
The counselling clinic cannot respond to emergencies, and messages to this assistant are not monitored in real time.

## If you are in crisis
If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local crisis line or emergency services now, or go to the nearest emergency department.

## Same-day appointments
A small number of same-day appointments are kept for urgent situations. Call the clinic reception when it opens at 9am to ask for one.
