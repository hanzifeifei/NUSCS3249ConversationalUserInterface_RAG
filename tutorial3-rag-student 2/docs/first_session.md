# Your first session

## Before you come
You do not need to prepare anything. This pre-consultation chat collects a few short answers so your counsellor can get ready. You can skip any question.

## What happens in the first session
The first session is a conversation of about 50 minutes. Your counsellor will ask what brings you in, explain how confidentiality works, and agree with you on next steps. There is no test and no diagnosis in the first session.

## Choosing your counsellor
You can ask for a counsellor of a particular gender, or one who speaks Mandarin, Malay or Tamil as well as English. The clinic will try to arrange this, although it can mean a longer wait.

## In person or online
Sessions can be in person or by video call. In-person sessions take place on Level 2 of the Student Services Building, which has step-free access.
