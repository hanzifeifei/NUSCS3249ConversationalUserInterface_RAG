# Support between sessions

## Self-help resources
The clinic website has short guides on managing stress, building a sleep routine and preparing for exams. They are written by the counselling team and are free to download.

## Changing your appointment time
If your timetable changes, you can move your appointment to another open slot. Ask this assistant to check availability.

## Giving feedback
You can give feedback about the clinic at any time through the anonymous feedback form on the website. Feedback is read by the clinic manager, not by your counsellor.
