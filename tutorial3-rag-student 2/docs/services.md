# Services and workshops

## One-to-one counselling
Most students start with one-to-one counselling: short-term sessions focused on what matters most to you right now.

## Group workshops
The clinic runs free group workshops during the semester. The Exam Stress workshop runs on Thursdays at 4pm in weeks 9 to 12. The Sleep Better workshop runs on Tuesdays at 1pm. Workshops last one hour and you can sign up at the front desk or online.

## Peer support
Trained student volunteers run drop-in peer support sessions on Wednesdays from 12pm to 2pm. Peer supporters are not counsellors, but they are good listeners and know where to find help.

## Referrals
If you need longer-term or specialist support, your counsellor can refer you to services outside the university.
