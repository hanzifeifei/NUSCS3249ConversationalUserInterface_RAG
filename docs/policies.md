# Clinic policies

## Opening hours
The counselling clinic is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. It is closed on public holidays and during the university's winter closure.

## Cost
The first consultation is free for all enrolled students. After that, students can have up to six free counselling sessions each semester. If you need more support, your counsellor will talk with you about the options.

## Cancelling or rescheduling
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment, so the slot can go to another student. You can cancel by replying to your confirmation email or by asking this assistant.

## Arriving late
Sessions last 50 minutes and end on time. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the counsellor may ask to reschedule.

## Missed appointments
If you miss two appointments in a row without cancelling, the clinic will contact you before booking further sessions.
