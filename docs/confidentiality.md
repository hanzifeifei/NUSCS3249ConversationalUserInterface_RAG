# Confidentiality and your records

## What stays private
Counselling is confidential. What you share stays with the counselling team. The clinic does not tell your lecturers, your family or the university about your sessions without your written consent.

## When the clinic must share information
There are a few exceptions. If someone is at serious risk of harm, or if the law requires it, the clinic may share information to keep people safe. Your counsellor will explain this at your first session.

## Your records
Counselling records are kept separately from your academic records. They are never shown on your transcript and do not affect your grades or your visa.

## Letters for your faculty
If you need a letter for your faculty, for example to support an extension or special consideration, your counsellor can write one, but only with your consent and only with the details you agree to.
